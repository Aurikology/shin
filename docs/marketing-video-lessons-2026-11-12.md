# Five marketing videos applied to Pexi (2026-11-12)

Source: five videos on YouTube growth and personal brand, notes in the agent repo at
`C:\agent\docs\other\video-notes-2026-11-11\` (Jake Trinder, MrBeast, Ronny Mitchell, Philipp
Humm, Caleb Ralston's 6h course). This is advice to weigh, not a ruling; `RULINGS.md` still wins.

Labels: **video** = a speaker's claim, never shown with proof; **checked** = seen in this repo on
2026-11-11; **assumed** = inference here. Nothing below has been tested on real traffic.

Pexi is a mass-market consumer app, so it sits closer to MrBeast and a recurring entertainment
series than to Jake's business-buyer YouTube.

## The plan

1. **A recurring series, not one-off ads (MrBeast 5:08; Ralston 6:15; Ronny, video).** "Steal or
   Rip-off?" as a weekly episode: item and price on screen in the first 2 seconds, viewers guess in
   the comments, Pexi gives the verdict. Existing ad concepts are already in this shape:
   steal-or-ripoff, pexi-reacts, reel-rip-off-hunt (checked, `ads/`).
2. **The mascot is the personality; overpricing is the villain (Ronny 5:41, 8:16, video).** Same as
   the existing ruling "the hook is a capability plus a villain" (checked, `docs/decisions.md`).
3. **Strong claims the video delivers (MrBeast 4:37, video).** "This store charges 40% more for the
   same thing" only when the scan in the video shows it.
4. **Find topics with Jake's demand search, aimed at Canadian shoppers (Jake 2:04 to 10:29).**
   Candidates to test: "is Costco worth it", grocery prices in Canada, shrinkflation, store brand vs
   name brand (assumed; the search decides). Log outliers, their title format and structure, and
   the failures that used the same format.
5. **Many cheap reels first, then fewer better ones (Ralston's accordion 1:21, video).** Post fast
   on the borrowed audiences already decided on (checked, `docs/decisions.md`, "The video test runs
   on borrowed audiences"), score each with Ralston's benchmark sheet (post as a multiple of the
   median per format, measured at 11 days, median updated monthly), then put the effort into the 2
   or 3 formats that came out on top.
6. **A savings challenge for proof (Ronny 11:24, Ralston 2:20, video).** "I scanned everything in my
   cart for 30 days. Here's what I saved." Saved dollars are the credibility for a price app.
7. **One topic per channel (Jake 29:06).** Every Pexi video is about prices; an off-topic hit
   brings viewers who ignore the rest.

## How each video should be built

- **Opening (MrBeast 5:08, Jake 14:36):** the item, the price and the question in the first frame;
  match what the thumbnail or caption promised before anything else.
- **Mix (Ralston 3:18):** 70% proven formats, 20% tweaks, 10% experiments.
- **Monthly top-10% review (Ralston 1:46):** the 1 or 2 traits shared by all top posts go in every
  new one.
- **Audience involvement (Ralston 2:34):** let viewers vote on the next item, and stitch their own
  scans.
