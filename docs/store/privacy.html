Pexi Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-05. This policy covers version 1.0 of the Pexi iPhone app and the server it talks to.

Who we are

Pexi is published by Jamin Xu, an individual developer. Questions, access requests and deletion requests go to useshinapp@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Pexi has no account, no sign-in, and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment details.
  • Your phone makes a random identifier for itself the first time you open Pexi. Pexi's server uses it to recognise the same phone on later requests. It is not your name, but everything Pexi records is tied to it.
  • Pexi records what you scan or type, what it answered, and how you use the app.
  • Location is off until you turn it on, and when it is on Pexi sends only a rough area about one kilometre wide.
  • This version of Pexi does not upload photos or camera images.
  • If you subscribe to Pexi Plus, Apple processes the payment and RevenueCat, a subscription service, records the purchase against the same random identifier. Pexi never sees your card or Apple ID details.
  • Pexi does not show ads, does not use advertising or analytics companies (RevenueCat is used only to process subscription purchases), and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What Pexi collects

A random device identifier. Your phone generates it and keeps it on the phone, together with a second random secret that proves later requests come from the same phone. Both are sent to Pexi's server with the requests the app makes. If you delete the app, the identifier is lost and a reinstall gets a new one.

What you scan or type. When you scan a barcode, Pexi sends the barcode digits. When you type a product name, Pexi sends the text you typed. While the camera is open, Pexi's app reads text that is visible in the camera view using text recognition that runs on your phone, and sends the recognised lines of text (not the image) to Pexi's server to find matching products. These are stored with the answer Pexi gave, whether Pexi answered or could not, any correction you made, and the time.

Prices, stores and ratings you enter. If you type the price you saw on a shelf, choose a store, tell Pexi a verdict was wrong, or rate a verdict, that is stored with the scan.

How you use the app. Pexi records when the app is opened, which screens you visit, buttons you tap, errors the app hits, and text you type into a field, including text you type and then delete without sending. This is recorded whether or not you have turned location on.

Technical details of each request. The app version, the platform, how long an answer took, and your language and market setting. Pexi's server also logs, for every request, the time, the web address requested, the response status, your IP address, the country the request came from, and your phone's user agent text.

Purchase history, only if you subscribe. Pexi Plus is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. The purchase is made with Apple, and RevenueCat records which plan you bought, when it renews and whether it is active, under your random device identifier as the app user identifier. Your phone also tells Pexi's server whether Pexi Plus is active, so the weekly scan limit can be lifted. Opening the Pexi Plus screen or tapping Restore Purchases contacts RevenueCat and Apple.

Rough location, only if you turn it on. Location is off by default. If you turn it on, your phone works out your position and rounds it on the phone to a grid square about one kilometre wide. Only the grid square is sent. Pexi's server stores the grid square, not your exact position, and discards any exact coordinates a request might carry. Pexi uses the grid square to suggest nearby shops, and the shop you pick is stored with the scan.

What Pexi does not collect in this version

  • Photos and camera images. The camera is used on your phone to read barcodes and text. Images are not uploaded.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or payment information.
  • Your exact location.
  • Advertising identifiers.

Why Pexi uses it

  • To answer your scan and to look up nearby shops.
  • To work out whether a price is good, using prices Pexi holds, including prices that people have entered. Prices you enter can be used in answers shown to other shoppers.
  • To improve Pexi's own price data and answers, including by using collected scans to train and tune Pexi's own price estimates.
  • To find and fix problems, using the usage events, errors and server logs described above.

Who else receives data

Pexi's server sends limited information to these outside services to answer a scan. None of them receives your device identifier. The only one that receives anything about where you are is the shop-finding service described below, and only when location is on.

  • Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) receives a barcode number when Pexi's own catalogue does not know the product, to look up the product's name, brand and size.
  • Anthropic (the maker of the Claude AI model) receives a product's name, brand, size, category and country when Pexi has no price for it, so the model can estimate a typical price range. It does not receive images, your device identifier or your location. The estimate is generated by an AI model and is saved by Pexi.
  • OpenStreetMap's Overpass service receives the centre point of your rough grid square, only when location is on, to find shops nearby.
  • Cloudflare carries traffic between the app and Pexi's server, so it handles your requests in transit.
  • Apple provides the App Store, handles downloads, and processes Pexi Plus payments. Pexi receives no card or Apple ID details from Apple.

RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) is different from the list above: it is contacted by the app itself, not by Pexi's server, and only for Pexi Plus. It receives your random device identifier as the app user identifier, together with the purchase information from Apple described under purchase history.

Pexi does not sell your data and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

How long Pexi keeps it

Pexi has no automatic deletion schedule for scan records, usage events, ratings, or server logs. They are kept until you ask for them to be deleted. Deleting the app removes the identifier from your phone but does not delete what the server already holds.

Your choices and your rights

  • You can leave location off, or turn it off at any time on the You screen. Turning it off stops new rough locations being sent. It does not remove ones already stored.
  • You can ask to see, or delete, the records tied to your device. Open the You screen and tap Delete my data, which prepares an email to useshinapp@gmail.com that includes your device identifier so the right records can be found, or write to that address yourself. Deletion is done by hand.
  • If you think a price or verdict is wrong, you can say so in the app by answering the question about whether the verdict was right.

Security

The app talks to Pexi's server over an encrypted HTTPS connection. No system is perfectly secure, and Pexi cannot promise that data will never be accessed without permission.

Children

Pexi is for people who are at least 18 years old, as the Terms of Use say. It is not directed at children, and Pexi does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used Pexi, email the address above and the records tied to that device will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If Pexi changes what it collects or who receives it, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. The version at this address is the current one.
