Pexi Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-21. This policy covers version 1.0 of the Pexi app for iPhone and Android and the server it talks to.

Who we are

Pexi is published by Jamin Xu, an individual developer. Jamin Xu is the person in charge of the protection of personal information for Pexi, including for the purposes of Quebec's privacy law. Questions, access requests and deletion requests go to him at usepexiapp@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Pexi has no account, no sign-in, and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment details.
  • Your phone makes a random identifier for itself the first time you open Pexi. Pexi's server uses it to recognise the same phone on later requests. It is not your name, but everything Pexi records is tied to it.
  • Pexi records what you scan or type, what it answered, and how you use the app.
  • Location is off until you turn it on, and when it is on Pexi sends only a rough area about one kilometre wide.
  • This version of Pexi does not upload photos or camera images.
  • Product names go to Anthropic's Claude AI because you agreed on the first screen when you opened Pexi. You can turn this off at any time on the You screen; when it is off, Pexi answers with a broad-guess range instead.
  • Pexi deletes what it records on a stated schedule. See "How long Pexi keeps it" below.
  • If you subscribe to Pexi Plus, Apple (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android) processes the payment and RevenueCat, a subscription service, records the purchase against the same random identifier. Pexi never sees your card, Apple ID or Google account details.
  • Pexi does not show ads, does not use advertising or analytics companies (RevenueCat is used only to manage subscriptions), and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What Pexi collects

A random device identifier. Your phone generates it and keeps it on the phone, in the phone's secure storage (the Keychain on iPhone, Android's encrypted storage on Android) and in the app's own storage, together with a second random secret that proves later requests come from the same phone. Both are sent to Pexi's server with the requests the app makes. On iPhone the Keychain can outlast the app, so reinstalling Pexi on the same phone keeps the same identifier and your scan count. On Android, deleting the app normally removes the identifier, so a reinstall usually starts with a new one.

What you scan or type. When you scan a barcode, Pexi sends the barcode digits. When you type a product name, Pexi sends the text you typed. While the camera is open, Pexi's app reads text that is visible in the camera view using text recognition that runs on your phone, and sends the recognised lines of text (not the image) to Pexi's server to find matching products. These are stored with the answer Pexi gave, whether Pexi answered or could not, any correction you made, and the time.

Prices, stores and ratings you enter. If you type the price you saw on a shelf, choose a store, tell Pexi a verdict was wrong, or rate a verdict, that is stored with the scan. When you type a price or pick a product, Pexi also stores a scrambled one-way code of your network (never your IP address) to keep fake prices out, and removes it together with your device identifier.

How you use the app. Pexi records when the app is opened, which screens you visit, buttons you tap, errors the app hits, and text you send, such as a search. Text you delete or leave unsent is not recorded; Pexi records only that a search was left unfinished. This is recorded whether or not you have turned location on.

Your agreement. The first screen asks for one agreement, to the Terms, this policy, Pexi recording what you scan and how you use the app, and product names going to Claude. Pexi records that you agreed and when, and if you later turn the Claude or usage switch off on the You screen, when you did.

Technical details of each request. The app version, the platform, how long an answer took, and your language and market setting. Pexi's server also logs, for every request, the time, the web address requested, the response status, your IP address, the country the request came from, and your phone's user agent text.

Purchase history, only if you subscribe. Pexi Plus is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system on iPhone and Google Play Billing on Android. The purchase is made with Apple or Google, and RevenueCat records which plan you bought, when it renews and whether it is active, under your random device identifier as the app user identifier. The app checks your subscription with RevenueCat each time it opens, and Pexi's server asks RevenueCat whether Pexi Plus is active for your device identifier, so the weekly scan limit can be lifted. Tapping Restore Purchases contacts RevenueCat and Apple or Google.

Rough location, only if you turn it on. Location is off by default. If you turn it on, your phone works out your position and rounds it on the phone to a grid square about one kilometre wide. Only the grid square is sent. Pexi's server stores the grid square, not your exact position, and discards any exact coordinates a request might carry. Pexi uses the grid square to suggest nearby shops, and the shop you pick is stored with the scan.

What Pexi does not collect in this version

  • Photos and camera images. The camera is used on your phone to read barcodes and text. Images are not uploaded.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or payment information.
  • Your exact location.
  • Advertising identifiers.

Why Pexi uses it

  • To answer your scan and to look up nearby shops.
  • To work out whether a price is good, using prices Pexi holds, including prices that people have entered. Prices you enter can be used in answers shown to other shoppers.
  • To improve Pexi's own price data and answers, including by using collected scans to train and tune Pexi's own price estimates.
  • To find and fix problems, using the usage events, errors and server logs described above.

Who else receives data

Pexi's server sends limited information to these outside services to answer a scan. Open Food Facts, Anthropic and OpenStreetMap's Overpass service do not receive your device identifier. Cloudflare, which carries your requests, and RevenueCat, described below, do. The only one that receives anything about where you are is the shop-finding service described below, and only when location is on.

  • Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) receives a barcode number when Pexi's own catalogue does not know the product, to look up the product's name, brand and size.
  • Anthropic (the maker of the Claude AI model), which you agree to on the first screen, receives a product's name, brand, size, category and country when Pexi has no price for it, so the model can estimate a typical price range. If you typed the name and Pexi did not recognise it, your typed words are the name it receives. It does not receive images, your device identifier or your location. The estimate is generated by an AI model and is saved by Pexi. You can turn this off at any time on the You screen; turning it off stops new requests, and Pexi answers with a broad-guess range instead.
  • OpenStreetMap's Overpass service receives the centre point of your rough grid square, only when location is on, to find shops nearby.
  • Cloudflare carries traffic between the app and Pexi's server, so it handles your requests in transit, including the device identifier they carry.
  • Apple provides the App Store, handles downloads, and processes Pexi Plus payments on iPhone. Pexi receives no card or Apple ID details from Apple.
  • Google provides Google Play, handles downloads, and processes Pexi Plus payments on Android. Pexi receives no card or Google account details from Google.

RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) is different from the list above: the app contacts it each time it opens, and Pexi's server contacts it to check whether Pexi Plus is active. It receives your random device identifier as the app user identifier, together with the purchase information from Apple or Google described under purchase history.

Pexi does not sell your data and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Same protection from every service. Pexi shares user data only with the services named here, and each of them provides the same or equal protection of that data as this policy states and as Apple's App Store Review Guidelines and Google Play's policies require.

Outside your province. Pexi's server and most of these services are outside Quebec, and some are outside Canada, including in the United States and the European Union, where privacy law differs. Pexi limits what each one receives to what is listed above.

How long Pexi keeps it

  • Server request logs: 90 days, then deleted.
  • Usage events: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Scans, ratings and prices you entered: after 24 months your device identifier is removed from them. What remains is a product, a price or an answer that is no longer tied to your phone, and Pexi keeps it to answer other shoppers.

Backups of the server can hold a copy for longer until they are replaced. You can ask for deletion sooner, as described below. Deleting the app does not delete what the server already holds, and on an iPhone the identifier stays in the Keychain, so a reinstall brings it back. On Android, a reinstall usually starts with a new identifier, and the records tied to the old one stay on the server until they are deleted on the schedule above. To have the records tied to your device deleted, use Delete my data on the You screen.

Your choices and your rights

  • You can leave location off, or turn it off at any time on the You screen. Turning it off stops new rough locations being sent. It does not remove ones already stored.
  • You can change your choice about Claude at any time on the You screen.
  • You can switch usage tracking off on the You screen. When it is off, Pexi stops recording how you use the app (opens, screens visited and taps).
  • You can ask to see, or delete, the records tied to your device. Open the You screen and tap Delete my data, which prepares an email to usepexiapp@gmail.com that includes your device identifier so the right records can be found, or write to that address yourself. Deletion is done by hand.
  • If you think a price or verdict is wrong, you can say so in the app by answering the question about whether the verdict was right.

How Pexi handles requests, complaints and incidents

These are the steps Pexi follows, in plain words. Jamin Xu, named above, is responsible for them.

  • Requests to see or delete your data. Use Delete my data on the You screen, or email the address above. Because Pexi holds no name or account, it finds your records by your device identifier, and it checks that the request comes from the person holding that phone before it shows or deletes anything. Pexi answers in writing within 30 days. A deletion removes the records tied to your device from Pexi's server, and the purchase record at RevenueCat if you subscribed. Server backups drop the copy within about 15 days. If you keep using the app on that phone, new records start again under the same identifier.
  • Complaints. Write to the address above. Pexi reads each complaint and answers in writing. You can also complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca) or to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).
  • Incidents. If personal information is ever lost, or seen or used by someone who should not have it, Pexi acts at once to stop it and writes the incident in a register, whether or not anyone was harmed. If an incident creates a risk of serious harm, Pexi tells the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec and the Privacy Commissioner of Canada promptly, and tells the people affected through a notice at the top of this page and in the app, because Pexi holds no email address or phone number for its users.

Security

The app talks to Pexi's server over an encrypted HTTPS connection. No system is perfectly secure, and Pexi cannot promise that data will never be accessed without permission.

Children

Pexi is for all ages, as the Terms of Use say. It asks no one for a name, email address, birth date or account, and it collects the same information described above from every user, whatever their age. A parent or guardian who wants a child's information deleted can email the address above, and the records tied to that device will be deleted. Location is off until you turn it on; if you are under 14, please ask a parent or guardian before you turn it on or share a price you typed.
