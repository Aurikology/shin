What Pexi is

Pexi is a price-checking app for shoppers. Point your phone's camera at a barcode, or type a product name, and Pexi tells you whether the price is great, good, reasonable or bad compared with the prices it holds. Where Pexi has no price for a product, it may show an estimated range generated by an AI model. All prices and estimates are information only.

Contact

For questions, bug reports, or requests about your data, email useshinapp@gmail.com.

Common questions

  • A price or verdict looks wrong. After a scan, answer the question about whether the verdict was right, or email us with the product and the store.
  • Deleting my data. Open the You screen in the app and tap Delete my data, which prepares an email to us, or write to the address above. Say which device you used if you are not using the app's button.
  • Pexi cannot answer. The app needs an internet connection and Pexi's server to answer. If you see no answer, try again in a few minutes.
  • Is Pexi free? Yes, version 1.0 is free to use. Free use includes 10 barcode scans a week. Typed searches are free and not counted. Pexi Plus, an optional subscription, gives 100 barcode scans a week. When the week's scans are used, they start coming back as the earliest ones pass seven days.

Pexi Plus: cancelling, restoring and refunds

  • Cancelling. Pexi Plus renews automatically until you cancel it. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Pexi, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least a day before the renewal date. You keep Pexi Plus until the end of the period you paid for.
  • A new phone, or Plus not showing. In Pexi, open the You screen, tap Pexi Plus, then Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.
  • Refunds. Apple takes the payment for Pexi Plus, and Pexi never receives your card or Apple ID details, so refunds are requested from Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose "Request a refund" and pick the Pexi Plus charge. Apple decides refund requests. If something went wrong with Pexi Plus, please also email us at the address above so we can fix it.
