Shin will be available to the entire world and not focused on canada. That means that all the systems we build have to be scalable and applicable to the world and not customized for canada. Like I said multiple times, having products without prices are useless, having shin identify something and not giving a price is wasting a users time. Read the comments in barcode to verdict tab to see how user data will be collected. users will have their price, a photo of the image or a barcode of the product, the price they input, a verdict of how good the price is, and a calculated median from gemini which is not stored but can be calculated from the information we have like i explained in the barcode to verdict tab. This data can be used in two ways: 1. (if the product already exists in our catalogue, the data from the user can be attached to that specific product(All data from users should naturally be taken with a grain of salt and not fully trusted. Furthermore, items should not be matched based on quantity and should be converted to the units of comparison used in this app. However, the original units should alos be stored. It can also be considered that the catalogue might have the same product but in two different quantities, this means that whichever quantity the users' item is closer to is the item that the users' data should be attached to.)) 2. (if a product doesn't exist in our catalogue then a user scan will add a new item to the catalogue with all of its information. This new item should also be taken with a grain of salt and not fully trusted, however, the median number many be a more valuable asset towards our catalogue. Our catalogue needs a way to sort similar incoming products as user scans might be slightly different for the same product. )
- while the camera is open, is it possible to get shin to continuous collect data not just on the item its focused on but on all the items around it. Espeically on store shelves, the product tag will contain the price of the item, the name of the item, and in the same photo frame, the item will also be there. This is very valuable data.

What this means is that for rules that gemini created on their website, claude automatically built complex systems around it to ensure everything is followed. This shows a massive problem with the priority of this repo. nothing should hold higher precedency than the words of the founder.
