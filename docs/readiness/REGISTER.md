## Summary

**What "ready" means (from the brief):** we find out by alarm or email, someone owns it, a written response exists, and it has been tried once; or the risk is accepted in writing with its named cost. Every item below is short of at least one of these, so no item is "ready".

Tiers: 1 = takes Pexi down or makes it unusable for everyone; 2 = loses money or paying users (including Apple-account events that stop sales); 3 = law, Apple account or data only; 4 = slow or small. Items that fit more than one tier sit in the lowest-numbered tier they reach. Within a tier the rank is likelihood (high 3, medium 2, low 1) times impact (1 to 5, my judgement of the named cost), then the cheapest fix first.

**Needs Jamin:** 52 of 93 items need Jamin himself (his accounts, money, a decision, his phone or the Mac); 41 can be done by a session or Aurik.

## Rehearsal plan (things that must be tried once)

Order matters: do the alarm drill first, because every other drill relies on being told when something goes wrong. Do the drills before launch while no real shoppers depend on the server, or in the quietest hour after. None of these steps was run in this pass; commands and file names come from mac/README.md, mac/DEPLOY.md and docs/privacy/data-requests.md and are not re-verified here. Jamin must approve each drill that touches the live server, his accounts or his phone.

### 1. Alarm drill (do first, everything else relies on it)

- **Covers:** R02, R03, R05, R17
- **What to do:** After the second watcher is set up: (1) note the clock time, stop the server job on the Mac (unload com.shin.server so KeepAlive does not restart it). (2) Record when each signal arrives: Lookout/ntfy on the PC, the deploy checker mail, the second watcher push on both phones, the GitHub alarm email. (3) Start the server, then repeat with only the tunnel stopped (the HTTP 530 case). (4) Run the GitHub alarm by hand (workflow_dispatch exists) against a deliberately wrong address and keep the email.
- **Who must be present:** Jamin at the Mac: yes (or Tailscale to it). Aurik: yes, phone on, told in advance.
- **Time:** 20 minutes hands-on; up to 9 hours elapsed if you also wait to see the GitHub schedule fire (it ran every 8 to 9 hours on 11-16 to 18)
- **What proves it worked:** A table of times. Pass: a phone push to BOTH founders within 5 minutes of the stop, for both the server stop and the tunnel stop; server back and public health returns 200; the wrong-address test produced an email.

### 2. Mac reboot and recovery

- **Covers:** R04, R08, R55
- **What to do:** At a quiet hour, with no App Review pending: (1) tell Aurik. (2) Restart the Mac and do not log in. (3) Time how long until https://relay.anjiawenda.com/api/health answers 200 from a phone on cellular. (4) If it never returns, log in, note which jobs were missing (backup, checker, hammer, server, tunnel), apply auto-login or move the jobs to LaunchDaemons, and repeat.
