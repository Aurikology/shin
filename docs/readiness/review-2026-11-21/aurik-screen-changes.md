# Your screens, what changed on 2026-11-21 and why

Jamin's word, 2026-11-21: "do all the recommended". Lane app-small of the readiness review. One line each.

- **Plus screen heading** is now "Check every price in your cart"; the 100 a week moved into the Plus step ("100 barcode scans every week, enough to check your whole cart"). Why: it sold a count, not a result (business rank 5).
- **Plus screen, Free step** no longer says "Typing a name is always free". Typed searches are still free and uncounted (ruling unchanged), and "Type a name instead" stays on the limit-spent screen. Why: the plans screen told shoppers how not to pay (business rank 4).
- **Limit-spent Plus screen** says "Check every price in your cart with Pexi Plus: 100 barcode scans a week." under the count (new key `paywall_buys`).
- **Seller line, renewal terms, Restore, Manage or cancel row**: unchanged, word for word.
- **"Your data" screen** no longer shows on first launch while photo identification is off (it had no switch, only text); first launch is now Agree, Permissions, Camera. Its point is one line on the Agree screen: "Your scans and the prices you add help Pexi learn and answer other shoppers." Why: a no-choice screen that read as a warning (business rank 3); no ruling requires the screen, and the line covers Quebec's notice at collection. With photos on, the screen comes back as before.
- **Camera banner**: the "Early access" badge is gone and the banner reads "Made for your everyday groceries / Pexi knows everyday groceries best, with new prices every week and more products on the way." Why: Apple 2.2 says betas belong on TestFlight (blind 9). Your point is kept. The only camera.js change is the two badge lines.
- **Scans left**: from 3 free scans left, a quiet line at the foot of the answer sheet, "3 free scans left this week · Get Pexi Plus", which opens Plus. Never for Plus, never with no limit, no pop-up. It lives in `scans-left.js` and draws into `.sheet` from outside camera.js. Why: the limit appeared only as a refusal at the shelf (business rank 7).
- **Answer wording**: "Pexi's broad guess for items like this" and "Pexi's AI estimate for this item" replace lines that opened with "no prices". Why: about 3 in 4 answers opened by saying what Pexi lacks (business rank 10).
- **You screen, Help and contact row**: opens a mail to the support address with the app version. Why: no way to write to us from inside the app (ops 7).
- **You screen, Share usage data switch** (on by default): off stops the app queueing or sending usage events and drops what was queued. Why: Apple 5.1.1(ii) and Quebec guidance want a way to say no (legal 7, apple-rules 8).
- **You screen and Plus screen, Terms and Privacy** open the French pages for French shoppers. Why: blind 14, ops 18.
- **You screen, "No scan limit."** is shown only when the server says there is no limit. Why: it was shown while the 10-a-week limit is on.
- **Developer section** (screen tags, pack read that sends a camera frame to Claude, text-match picker) does not exist inside the iPhone or Android app at all; a browser tab still has it. Why: Apple 2.5.19 bars dormant features (apple-rules 9).
- **French**: tu throughout (the Agree screen, the Claude switch, "Ton signalement", "la page Toi"); "scan" as the one word (no numérisation); a no-break space before every ? and : in the French tables and Pexi's French lines; the machine-like strings rewritten (Prix médian, Peu de données, Saisi, pouces levés, Certain, and others); shelf is "tablette", seller is "marchand". Why: ops 14 to 17. Onboarding still writes PEXI in capitals, as the English does (onboarding is off).
- **Store listing** (`docs/store/listing.md`): name "Pexi: Grocery Price Checker", subtitle "Scan it. Know if it's a deal.", a description that leads with what the shopper gets, no "typical prices in Canada", and a caption for each of the five screenshots to recapture. Why: business 1, 2, 11; premortem 2.
