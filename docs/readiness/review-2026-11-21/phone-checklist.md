# Pexi phone check (about 30 minutes, one iPhone)

Use the newest TestFlight build. Purchases in TestFlight are free test purchases: nothing is charged.
For each line write OK, or what went wrong plus a screenshot. Do it in a real store if you can,
otherwise with products at home.

## Fresh install
1. Delete Pexi if installed, install from TestFlight, open it. Note how long until the camera is usable.
2. Go through the first screens. Say Yes to "send product names to Claude". Allow camera. Deny location.
3. Scan 10 different products by tapping each green outline. For each: did an answer appear, did it have a
   price, did the verdict make sense? (Count: answered __ / 10, with price __ / 10.)
4. Type 3 product names: one exact, one misspelled, one nonsense word.
5. Open the price pad on one product ("Is this a good price?"), enter a price, check the verdict.

## Limit and Plus
6. Keep scanning until the free limit stops you. Does the paywall appear, is the price shown,
   does the wording make you want to pay?
7. Buy Plus (monthly). Does scanning work again straight away?
8. Delete the app, reinstall from TestFlight. Does it still know you have Plus without tapping anything?
   Then tap Restore Purchases: does it confirm?
9. If you have a second iPhone or iPad on the same Apple ID: install there, tap Restore. Plus works?

## Hard conditions
10. Airplane mode, scan once: what does it say? Turn data back on: does it recover by itself?
11. Settings > General > Language: French. Reopen Pexi: is everything in French?
12. Turn on VoiceOver, try to scan and hear the answer. Can you do it without looking?
13. Largest text size (Settings > Display > Text Size): does any screen cut off or overlap?
14. Leave the app in the background 10 minutes, come back: does it pick up where it was?

## Anything else
15. Anything that looked broken, slow, ugly, confusing or still says "Shin": screenshot it.
