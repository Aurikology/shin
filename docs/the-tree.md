# The tree

The root is the moonshot. Going down a level means "what has to exist for the thing above it to
exist". Read a line upward and it says what that piece unlocks; read it downward and it says
what it is waiting on.

**This is the skeleton pass, 2026-09-06: the root and the two levels under it, drawn from the
four documents only** (the vision, the combined pipeline, the moonshot, the defect log), as the
first of the passes the tree rules set out. Nothing here is marked built or not built, because
that marking is a later pass that reads the code rather than the write-ups. Every second-level
line therefore ends with **not decomposed yet** unless it is already one of the things only he
can decide, or a wall, or blocked by someone outside.

**How to read a line.** Each one says what it is in plain words, then four things: the **kind**
of item it is about, the **goal number** its subtree moves, where it **comes from**, and its
**state** in this pass. A number on a line says whether it was counted, derived, or is an
untested target.

**The three goal numbers** are the ones in the vision: **want** (installs, installs per video,
who comes back in week two), **reliance** (how often it answers per kind, scans per returning
person, corrections per hundred answers), **money** (downloads, payers per hundred downloads,
what each payer leaves, savings measured rather than claimed).

## What this pass did not do

- **No piece is marked built or not built.** That marking reads the code and opens every citation,
  and it is the pass after the branches.
- **No branch is decomposed.** Each of the second-level lines still has to be broken down until
  every end of it is one of six things: running, written with nothing calling it, buildable in
  one sitting, blocked by someone outside, his to decide, or a wall.
- **The ways around the four walls are named but not drawn.** Each wall's children are its way
  around, and the sufficiency question gets asked of the way around rather than of the wall.
- **Order is not in here.** The tree gives what waits on what. What gets done first comes from
  the vision: what cannot be taken back, then the instruments, then whatever moves a goal number
  most.
