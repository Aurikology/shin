What is wrong now

Four faults, read off the running app

Not opinion. These came from opening the build at localhost and looking at it.

It narrates itself instead of being itself
The home screen contains the sentence The camera keeps the top of the screen while the collection is small. There is no camera on it. A description of an interface is not an interface.
Scaffolding shipped as product
STAGE 04 · THE FIRST SCAN sits at the top of the scan screen, along with a STAGES and a THEME button in the app bar. These are tools for building the thing, printed on the thing.
The verdict is a box, not a moment
The single instant the product exists for is a small rounded rectangle above three paragraphs of serif body copy, and it overflows the fold mid sentence. Everything on screen carries the same weight, so nothing carries any.
Colour carries almost no load
For a product whose entire output is a three way judgment, the tiers appear only as low saturation tints. Worse, the brand pink and the walk-away red are close enough in hue to be confused in a screenshot, which is exactly where the brand colour is supposed to do its work.

The system

Three laws, each traceable to a constraint

A rule that traces to none of these is decoration and gets deleted.

LAW 1

The camera is the app

Default screen is a live viewfinder. The shutter is the one control always inside a thumb's reach, dead centre at the bottom, because centre bottom is the only position reachable from either grip. Scan, identify and verdict are one surface with three states, never three pages.

LAW 2

Hue is the verdict, saturation is the confidence

The output is a judgment carrying an uncertainty, so colour has to carry both. A confident walk away is a solid saturated field. A thin one is the same hue, hollow and outlined. Calibration becomes something you see rather than something a footnote claims.

LAW 3

A refusal is a screen, not an error

Five of seven known items refuse, so refusal is the most common outcome of the primary action. It is grey, never red, because red means the price is bad and must never be read as no data. It always hands back exactly one thing to do.

Colourspace

Four verdict states, and a brand colour that is never one of them

Each verdict has a base for chrome and a brighter variant for use over a live camera feed in a well lit store. Text colour on each field is not a preference, it is the only pairing that clears contrast.

Type

Three faces, one job each

The reading serif is removed from the product surface entirely. It is the single biggest reason the current app reads as a document. Everything Shin asserts is a measurement, and measurements want a confident technical voice.

Bricolage Grotesque 800 · prices and the verdict word only

$1.47

Tabular figures always. A price that changes width as it updates looks like a bug and undermines the one thing being measured.

Instrument Sans · every piece of UI

Watch this price Tell me when it drops below the usual $1.47 and I will let you know.

Labels, buttons, rows, sheets. Two lines maximum on the verdict surface.

IBM Plex Mono · provenance only

METRO · 2026‑09‑03
LOBLAWS · 2026‑09‑03
NO FRILLS · 2026‑09‑02

Sellers, dates, counts. Mono signals a recorded measurement, and honesty about sources is the product.

The face

Six expressions, three personalities, chosen by the user

The face is the verdict, not a decoration in the corner of a card. Every face is drawn as SVG carrying an xmlns, so it survives being rasterised onto a share card. That is a defect this project already found once.

The attitude is the user's call, and that was your decision, not mine.

It turns the riskiest tone choice into an onboarding moment and gives the video test something worth screenshotting, since which Shin someone has is itself the joke. The honest cost, recorded rather than buried: every user-facing string now exists three times, and the face system carries three variants of six expressions. Default at first run is Deadpan, because a price tool that is wrong while being cute is worse than one that is wrong while being flat.

Motion

Motion exists to make the judgment feel like it landed

It never decorates. Under reduced motion every entry becomes an opacity fade of the same duration and nothing moves in space.

MomentWhat happens
Shutter pressScale to 0.92 over 90ms, ring pulses outward once
Frame freezeFeed stills, 6% desaturation over 120ms
ReticleContracts onto the found object over 200ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)
Verdict rises260ms from the bottom edge, same curve
Price numeralEnters at 0.94 scale and 0 opacity over 180ms, starting 60ms after the field
FaceExpression morphs over 220ms, eyes first, mouth 40ms behind
Confidence dotsFill left to right, 80ms each, after the numeral
RefusalSame rise but 340ms and grey, one slow blink. No shake, no buzz, no red.
One thing is forbidden outright.

Never animate a price counting up. A number moving through values it never had is a fabricated measurement, and it would be the prettiest way for this product to lie.
