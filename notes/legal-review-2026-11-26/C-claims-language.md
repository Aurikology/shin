# Legal review C: claims, language and consumer law (Pexi)

Prepared 2026-11-25 for a lawyer to check. Not legal advice. Paths are under C:/shin. "Verified" means I read the primary text through a fetch tool this session; "unverified" means I could not retrieve the primary text and the point comes from search snippets or memory and must be checked.

## 1. Facts (what shoppers see)

### 1.1 Price, accuracy and source claims
- Four verdict words: "Great / Good / Reasonable / Bad price" (`app/public/js/ui-strings.js:334-337`). Each verdict sheet carries a kicker "Pexi's estimate" (`ui-strings.js:330`; `screens/camera.js:1661+` distribution sheet, `camera.js:1879+` catalogue sheet).
- The range is shown as "Usually X to Y" (`ui-strings.js:409`) and "Pexi estimates the typical price at X, with 8 in 10 prices between X and Y" (`ui-strings.js:410-411`).
- A basis line under the range says where it came from: own prices at N stores (`ui-strings.js:277-279`), Statistics Canada average "adjusted to regular shelf prices" (`ui-strings.js:279-281`, `376`), similar products, across the border, "Pexi's AI estimate for this item" (`ui-strings.js:373`), "Estimated by AI" (`ui-strings.js:284, 288, 428, 442`), "A rough estimate for its category/aisle" (`ui-strings.js:374-375`), "Pexi's broad guess for items like this" (`ui-strings.js:285`). The basis line is in the peek of the catalogue sheet (`camera.js:1914-1918`, rendered in the peek block near `camera.js:1975`).
- Confidence words: "Certain", "Fairly sure", "Not fully confident" (`ui-strings.js` keys `vd_conf_high/medium/low`, near line 336-340). "Certain" is the same string in French (identical-string check on `vd_conf_high`).
- AI-named item: "Name read off the pack by AI" (`ui-strings.js:297`).
- "Sale prices are not in the estimate" (`ui-strings.js:383`).
- "The typical price is Pexi's estimate of what most shoppers pay" (`ui-strings.js:83`).
- Marketing-style lines: "Pexi knows everyday groceries best, with new prices every week and more products on the way" (`ui-strings.js:314`, also `guide_4_body` at 781); "Scan any product and instantly know if it's worth it" (`onboarding-strings.js:32`); "Know a good price before you pay" (`onboarding-strings.js:30`); "scan and compare local deals" and "store prices near you" (`onboarding-strings.js:184, 190`).

### 1.3 Store and brand names, logos
- Store names are plain text only: bundled chain list (Walmart, Costco, Metro, Sobeys, T&T, Best Buy, IKEA and others) in `app/public/js/chains.js:17+`; names appear next to prices in dot rows, the shop picker and the share card ("On the tag / Elsewhere", `ui-strings.js:715-719`). No logo files under `app/public` (a name search for "logo" and "brand" found none). Social brand names appear in the onboarding "where did you hear about us" list (`onboarding-strings.js:74-80`).
- Disclaimer: "Pexi is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any store, brand or data provider named in the app. Prices are what somebody recorded on the day shown beside them, not an offer, and not checked with the shop" (`app/public/js/legal/document.js:196`); Play draft line 45.

### 1.4 French availability
- The build is pinned to English: `FLAGS.languages = false` ("the French table and the language row. Off, the locale is pinned to English whatever the phone asks for", `app/public/js/flags.js:45-46, 83`; applied at `flags-boot.js:24`; his ruling 2026-09-21, `RULINGS.md:872`). The market is also pinned to Canada (`flags.js:84`). Git history of flags.js shows no later flip of `languages`.
- French tables exist and match the English by key count: ui-strings 771/771, onboarding 153/153, paywall 11/11 (node check). voice-fr has 150 line keys and a test enforces parity (`test/voice.test.mjs:148+`). They are not shown while the flag is off.
- Terms and privacy: one document with a full French version (`legal/document.js:273-512`, sections mirror English). Hosted as `docs/store/terms-fr.html` and `privacy-fr.html`. The in-app first-launch document follows the pinned locale, so it appears in English only (inferred from the pin; not run).
- Store listings: the Play draft and the App Store text are English only. `docs/store/listing.md:71` has French screenshot captions "for the French listing (gap 61)" but no French listing text exists. The Play draft says "Prices judged in your own market, with English and French throughout" (`play-listing-draft-2026-11-26.md:31`), which the build does not do while both flags are off.
- French register: the French UI and terms use "tu" not "vous".
- The no-JavaScript fallback page is bilingual (`app/public/index.html:100-117`).

### 1.5 Terms: disclaimers and limits (`legal/document.js`, effective 2026-11-25)
- Information only; no guarantee of accuracy; "Always rely on the price displayed by the store"; not an offer or a promise a store will honour a price (`:80-85`).
- AI estimates generated by Anthropic's Claude; "Treat all AI-generated content as unverified" (`:87-93`).
- Allergens and ingredients from open databases "may be missing, out of date or wrong" (`:192`).
- "As is" warranty disclaimer "to the extent the law allows" (`:237-242`). Liability limit: "To the fullest extent the law where you live allows ... not liable for any loss ... including a purchase made or not made because of it", with a Quebec carve-out for "the consequences of its own act" (`:244-250`).
- "Pexi may change, suspend or stop at any time"; Plus changes need 30 days' notice, with a Quebec notice showing new and old clause and cancel up to 30 days after (`:252-257`).
- Governing law Ontario, consumer protections preserved (`:259-263`).
- User contributions licence: perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, may be published (`:180-185`).
- Acceptance: "Tapping Accept and continue, or using Pexi, means you agree" (`:38`); shown once at first launch, re-readable from Settings (`screens/agree.js`).

### 1.6 Accessibility basics
- Viewport allows zoom (`index.html:15`, no user-scalable or maximum-scale); `lang` set per locale (`lib/locale.js:128+`); 82 aria attributes in camera.js; 21 reduced-motion CSS matches; a VoiceOver label test (`test/a11y-voiceover-labels.test.mjs`). The verdict is a word plus colour, not colour alone (`camera.js:1908`). I did not test contrast, text scaling or screen-reader use.

## 4. Top gaps by named cost
1. French: the build is pinned to English while Quebec consumers are targeted (R6-R9): Charter order, terms that may not bind, store takedown.
2. The Play draft says "English and French throughout" and "your own market" and the build does neither (R1): penalty for a false claim, listing rejection.
3. "Scan any product and instantly know" and "Certain" are unsupported performance wording (R2, R4): penalty up to $750,000 for an individual.
4. Legacy "Nobody else charges that" lines that name a seller, if reachable (R1, R13): penalty, retailer claim. Reachability not verified.
5. The "typical shopper" comparison, and any future savings figure, need a stated test on file (R2, R12): penalty, FTC.
