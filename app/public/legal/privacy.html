Pexi - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28 (beta). This page is kept in step with what the app actually does; when the app changes, this page is updated the same way.

Who we are

Pexi is a beta grocery and retail price-checking app. This policy covers Pexi's mobile app and its server. Contact us at useshinapp@gmail.com.

No account, no name, no email

Pexi does not ask for your name, email address, or an account. The first time you open Pexi, your device creates a random identifier for itself and sends it back on every scan afterward. That identifier tells us it is the same device asking again; it does not tell us who you are.

What we collect every time you use Pexi

Every scan you make (a barcode, a photo, or a name you typed) is recorded: what you scanned, what we told you, whether we answered, refused, or you corrected us, and when. We also record how you use the app itself: screens you open, buttons you tap, and text you type into a search box, including text you type and then delete without sending, because that is still something we learn from. If you tell us a verdict was wrong, or rate an answer good, fair, or great on your own scale, that is recorded too. None of this requires a name, an email, or a sign-up.

Photos: off until you turn them on, and what happens either way

Photo saving is off by default. Either way, if you take a photo to identify a product, it is sent once to Google's Gemini AI model to produce an answer.

  • Photo saving off (the default): your photo is read once, to produce that one answer, and is not kept afterward.
  • Photo saving on: the photo is kept, tied to that scan, so a wrong answer can be reviewed by a person and so Pexi can learn from it. We do not currently run an automatic deletion schedule on kept photos: a kept photo stays until you ask us to delete it, or until we turn on an automatic deletion window in the future, at which point this page will say so and give the number of days.

You can turn photo saving on at any time from the You screen; turning it off afterward does not delete a photo already kept from an earlier scan.

Location: off until you turn it on, and only ever a rough area

Location saving is off by default. If you turn it on:

  • Your phone asks the device's operating system for your exact GPS position and snaps it, on the phone, to a grid cell about one kilometre wide (never a street address or exact point).
  • To do that snap in a way we can check on our side, the app sends that exact reading to our server as part of the same request that carries the rough cell. Our server discards the exact reading immediately and only ever stores the rough, kilometre-wide cell; your precise coordinates are never written to our database, whatever a client sends.
  • We use the rough area to suggest nearby stores, and the store you pick is saved with that scan so prices can be tracked per store.

If location saving is off, none of this happens: your phone is never asked for a location reading at all.

Both toggles (photos and location) are independent, and you can change either one at any time from the You screen.

Why we collect this

We use what Pexi collects to answer your scans, to count your free scans, to decide what to look up next, and to notice when a nearby shopper already checked the same price. We also use collected scan data, including photos kept under the photo toggle, to train and improve Pexi's own price-answering system, and to answer other shoppers who scan the same or similar products. If you tell us an answer was wrong, that correction is used to fix it going forward.

When Pexi asks an AI model

A photo you take to identify a product is sent once to Google's Gemini AI model, whether or not you have chosen to let us keep a copy of it afterward. When Pexi has no price of its own for something you scanned or typed, we may also ask an AI model for a general, typical price range; that request sends only the product's name or category, never your photo, never your exact location, and never your device identifier tied to anything else about you.

Who sees this data

Nobody outside Pexi's own server sees your scan history, except the AI model providers named above, who receive only what this page says they receive, for the single purpose of answering that request.

How long we keep it

Scan and event records are kept for the life of the beta. A photo kept under the photo toggle is kept until you ask us to delete it, per the "Photos" section above; there is currently no automatic deletion schedule running by default.

How to see or delete your data

During the beta there is no in-app delete button. Email useshinapp@gmail.com and we will delete your device's scan records, saved photos, and saved location or store choices by hand.

Children

Pexi is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from a child under 13. If you believe a child has used Pexi, email us and we will remove what we can identify.

Changes to this policy

Because Pexi is a beta, this policy may change. The version at this URL is always the current one, and it is kept to match the wording on the app's own consent screen.
