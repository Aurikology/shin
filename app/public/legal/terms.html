4. Prices are estimates, not guarantees

Shin's verdicts (good, fair, or high) are estimates, built from Shin's own collected price data and, when Shin has no price of its own, from a general estimate produced by an AI model. Shin does not check every store, every region, or every moment in time, and a price can change after Shin last saw it. Shin is a decision aid, not a substitute for reading the actual price tag or receipt at the till. Do not rely on Shin as your only source before completing a purchase, and always take the store's own displayed or scanned price as the price you will actually be charged.
