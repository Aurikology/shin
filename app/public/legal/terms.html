1. What Pexi is

Pexi lets you scan a barcode, take a photo, or type a product name to see whether a price is good, fair, or high. Pexi is currently a beta: features change, break, or disappear without notice, and the service may be limited, paused, or discontinued at any time.

You must be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority where you live if that is older, to agree to these Terms and to use Pexi. By using Pexi you confirm that this is true. If you are not yet at that age, do not use Pexi.

3. Your account and device

Pexi does not require a name, email address, or sign-up. Your device gets a random identifier the first time you open the app; that identifier is how Pexi recognizes your device on later scans. If you lose your device or reinstall Pexi, that identifier is not recoverable and any free-scan count or purchase restoration follows the store account (Apple ID or Google account) that made the purchase, not the identifier itself.

4. Prices are estimates, not guarantees

Pexi's verdicts (good, fair, or high) are estimates, built from Pexi's own collected price data and, when Pexi has no price of its own, from a general estimate produced by an AI model. Pexi does not check every store, every region, or every moment in time, and a price can change after Pexi last saw it. Pexi is a decision aid, not a substitute for reading the actual price tag or receipt at the till. Do not rely on Pexi as your only source before completing a purchase, and always take the store's own displayed or scanned price as the price you will actually be charged.
